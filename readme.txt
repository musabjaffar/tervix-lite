A responsive, accessible news and magazine theme with a hero image, breaking news ticker, light and dark mode, share buttons and comments.

== Description ==
Tervix Lite is a classic theme for news sites, magazines and blogs. It includes a home page hero image, breaking news ticker, light and dark mode, social links, share buttons, threaded comments, related posts, author box, a banner colour for every category, and right-to-left support.

== Installation ==
1. Appearance > Themes > Add New > Upload Theme, choose tervix-lite.zip and activate.
2. Appearance > Customize > Tervix Options: set the hero image, headline, accent colour, ticker and social links.
3. Appearance > Menus: assign a menu to the Primary location.

== Sample content ==
On a brand new site, open Appearance > Customize. WordPress shows Tervix Lite's sample posts, pages, menu and widgets as a preview. Press Publish to keep them, or ignore them to start empty.

== Privacy ==
Tervix Lite does not track visitors, set cookies or contact external services. The light/dark choice is saved in the visitor's own browser (localStorage) and is never sent anywhere. Share buttons are plain links that open the network only when clicked.
